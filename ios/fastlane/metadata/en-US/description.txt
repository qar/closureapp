Papermoon plays the music you own, from your iPhone, the way it was recorded.

YOUR LIBRARY, ON YOUR IPHONE
• Import albums and folders from the Files app, Google Drive or Finder. Folders keep their layout.
• Every copy is checked before it joins your library, so an interrupted import never leaves half an album.
• Files with the same content are found and skipped, whatever their names.
• No account, no streaming, no ads. Your music plays with no connection.

SOUND AS RECORDED
• FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, AAC and MP3, up to 24-bit and high sample rates.
• Gapless playback for live albums and continuous mixes.
• No volume processing in the app. The output follows each album's sample rate when your device allows it.
• The Signal Path page shows what happens to the audio, step by step.

MADE FOR LISTENING
• Synced lyrics from .lrc files or the tags, or found online when you ask.
• Album info and covers from MusicBrainz when you ask. Your files are never changed.
• Playlists, Favorites, Play Next and Play Last.
• Play in order, repeat all, repeat one or shuffle. Playback continues where you left off.

Papermoon only goes online when you ask it to: to import from Google Drive, or to look up album info or lyrics.
